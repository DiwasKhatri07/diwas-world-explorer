# Diwas World Explorer — Full World Expansion

## Experience Promise

Diwas World Explorer becomes an **interactive isometric expedition portfolio**. Visitors do not enter a conventional homepage; they choose a route, learn navigation through a short guided ritual, and move across a growing island atlas that turns Diwas’s learning journey and public project archive into places to discover.

## World Structure

| Level | Role in the journey | Discovery focus | Visual identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **South Shore** | Starting world and tutorial | Diwas’s core profile, learning journey, navigation basics | Bright tropical island, field-notes UI, clear sand routes |
| **Data Observatory** | Project Archive I | NEPSE API as a developer-tool case study | High-altitude observatory, signal charts, navy/coral navigation instruments |
| **Builder’s Harbor** | Project Archive II | Magic Link, Diwas PyPi, and future work stations | Teal harbor, package crates, message beacons, modular dock paths |
| **Night Lab** | Skills and active learning | Python, Java, full-stack work, AI, and Nepali LLM exploration | Midnight blue technical grove, glowing terminals, archival blueprints |

## Interaction Modes

The world supports **Tap/Mouse Route** for click-to-move, **Keyboard Route** for WASD/arrow-key travel, and **Voice Route** as an opt-in browser feature. Voice Route is deliberately off until the visitor presses its control and approves the browser microphone request. The site does not activate camera or microphone access automatically.

Camera behavior is a game-camera feature rather than a device-camera requirement. Visitors can toggle **Expedition View** (wide isometric) and **Close Explorer View** (a tighter third-person-style framing). Switching modes creates a short camera-flick transition and visibly changes the player-marker scale and HUD density.

## Tutorial Flow

The entry menu launches a three-step tutorial: select a movement route, tap or press a key to send Diwas to a coral marker, then enter a landmark to open a field note. The tutorial is skippable, but it returns whenever a visitor opens the help command.

## Content Rules

Public information from the provided sites becomes project history and technical-learning context. The world excludes personal contact details, school details, precise location, visitor counters, and age. Real project visuals are used only when drawn from the user-provided public pages or an explicitly approved project asset. The player character remains a stylized explorer until Diwas uploads a photo or explicitly selects an approved public photo.
