# Assets — Diwas World Explorer

## Art Direction

**Postcard Archipelago:** a high-key, premium editorial adventure map rendered as a compact 3D island. The camera is elevated and oblique, with deep ocean teal water, faded turquoise shallows, parchment sand paths, soft palm greens, deep navy UI ink, and one active coral accent: **Compass Coral #FF715B**. Forms are rounded and highly legible; surface detail is hand-painted and restrained. The mood is warm, curious, and grounded—not neon, not pixel-art, and not photorealistic.
